01 · The problem

Murine models underpin preclinical vaccine research, yet their translational value remains contested. Prior cross-species comparisons were largely descriptive — gene-centric and disconnected from sequence evolution and cis-regulatory architecture. 201 mouse immunization datasets were screened from GEO and 6 matched mouse–human conditions carried forward.

02 · Design & harmonisation

  • Vaccination — Fluad® (influenza), Engerix B® (hepatitis B)
  • Infection — S. aureus, E. coli
  • Injury — burn, trauma-haemorrhage
  • + DMD (mdx mouse) as a biological negative control

Blood transcriptomes · GEO / BioProject · limma + empirical Bayes · covariates (age, sex, ethnicity) · human downsampled to n = 5, 200 iterations, median statistics.

03 · Granularity: cells disagree, pathways agree

Module level (BTM NES) · Pearson

ρ = 0.89–0.97

Gene-level effect sizes correlate only moderately; module activation is highly conserved. Shared leading-edge genes = 67.5–79% of mouse LEGs (70.5% pooled): the translational signal lives in the leading edge, not in all genes.

04 · Intensity & kinetics

Infection & injury — conserved

ρ = 0.41–0.59

Acute S. aureus, E. coli and burn engage evolutionarily stable programs; |ΔLog2FC| differences are smaller than in negative controls (WMW P < 1 × 10⁻¹⁵).

Single-dose vaccination — diverges

ρ = −0.32–0.27

Fluad® and Engerix B® stay near baseline; preclinical immunogenicity may need boosters or stronger adjuvants.

Timing ≠ chronology · mouse 2–6 h matches the human peak inflammatory window (days 1–3) → align by biological equivalence.

05 · Classification performance

Mouse signatures call human direction — best at module level

0.84–0.98

Module-level ROC-AUC; gene-level signatures in infection & injury 0.64–0.74; DMD negative control 0.48–0.61 (near random).

06 · Why genes diverge

  • Higher protein sequence identity → lower |ΔLog2FC| (ρ = −0.08)
  • Greater TF availability / sharing (ρ = −0.017 / −0.013)
  • Greater Kimura K80 distance → higher |ΔLog2FC| (ρ = 0.10)
  • Enhancer multiplicity, pELS / dELS (ρ = 0.036 / 0.039)
  • CRE turnover & CTCF-bound pELS (ρ = 0.042)

Small per-gene effects, but a consistent direction: sequence constraint tracks stability; regulatory plasticity tracks divergence.

07 · Multilayer modelling

Layer 1
DGE baseline
Layer 2
+ BTM modules
Layer 3
+ evolution
Layer 4
+ TF & CRE

leave-one-condition-out CV (4 folds) · 4 algorithms: linear/logistic · lasso · random forest · neural network.

Mouse rank alone R² = 0.001 and direction alone ROC-AUC 0.511 — no transfer. Adding the biological layers lifts the random forest to R² = 0.234 (human rank) and ROC-AUC = 0.849 (direction); shared-LEG lasso 0.617 / 0.627.

08 · Conclusions & availability

Translatability is not a fixed property of the mouse: it is a function of granularity, stimulus intensity and temporal alignment. Four out-of-fold scores — score_shared, score_rank, score_direction, score_translational — prioritise candidate genes.

Limitations: small murine cohorts; two inbred strains (C57BL/6, CB6F1); single-dose vs prime-boost; blood-only BTMs; one-to-one Ensembl orthologs.
